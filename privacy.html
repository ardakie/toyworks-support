ToyWorks Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

ToyWorks is a single-player puzzle game. We built it so that it does not need your personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. ToyWorks has no account, does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos, and contains no advertising or analytics software.

What is stored

Your progress (levels, stars, coins and settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

ToyWorks is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that use data, such as purchases or online play, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.
