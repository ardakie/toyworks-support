Common questions

How do I play? Drag a piece onto the board. Four or more touching blocks of the same colour pop and fill the goals at the top.

Does the game need the internet? No. ToyWorks plays fully offline.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device. It is removed if you delete the app.
